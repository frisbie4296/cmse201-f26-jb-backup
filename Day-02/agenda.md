# Prep work (note this is still required even if these are just loaner laptops)
- use CMSE account to install:
      - Zoom
      - Crestron AirMedia
      - register them to use MSU Net 3.0

# Before class
- Lead Instructor: If needed for remote students:
      - log into Zoom yourself and start the meeting
      - connect to the projector
      - open breakout rooms for virtual students
- for each table:
      - whiteboards + markers

- Notes:
      - IF you have remote students, when talking to the whole class, stand close enough to your computer that the mic picks you up
      - remote students should be in the main room at the start, and in their breakout rooms during group work
      - check in with remote students periodically

# Announcements: (2 min)
- reminder about office hours and the course calendar

# Go over the pre-class assignment (20 minutes)
- focusing on what was mentioned in the survey
- answering student questions live
- end with a discussion of 2.4, since it is a good transition to in-class

# In-class assignment (74 min)
      - read the learning goals (2 min)
      - read The Problem (2 min)
      	     - assign tables to problems
      - Part 1 as a group (30 min)
             - make sure people try out different values and get a range
      - Part 2 (30 min)
      - Collect results and compare. Did different groups get similar results? (10 min)
