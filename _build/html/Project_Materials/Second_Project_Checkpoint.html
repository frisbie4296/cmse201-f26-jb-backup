Second Project Checkpoint#

Summary#

Summarize the feedback you received from your classmates and/or instructor on your project. Respond to the feedback brought up. Your response should either be plans for how to implement the feedback or an explanation for why it is infeasible for you to act upon the feedback. You will submit a PDF of your summaries to D2L.

Summary of Lightning Presentation Feedback#

In a few (2-4) bullet points, summarize the main points of feedback that you received on your lightning presentation. An example of the both feedback and response is given below.#

  • Example Feedback: Some audience members had difficulty seeing how the plot in my presentation was connected to the research question I am trying to answer.

    • ExampleResponse: I realize now that the variables I was plotting–imported copper versus time–isn’t as relevant for answering my research question, “How has the growth of the semiconductor industry shaped the South Korean economy?” I will refocus that part of my work on the growth of metal mining in South Korea, as that has a more direct effect on the economy. I have already found the data I need for this analysis here (INSERT LINK TO NEW DATASET), now I just need to incorporate it into my analysis. Once I’ve imported the new dataset, I will slot it into section 3.3 of my project and use it for the rest of my analysis.

Summary of Project Notebook Feedback#

In a few (3-5) bullet points, summarize the main points of feedback that you received on your project notebook. An example of the both feedback and response is given below.#

  • Example Feedback 1: Plots that I made were very informative, but plotting code in section 3.4 was very long and repetitive. Suggested using function or for loop.

    • Example Response: I agree that code here is quite repetitive, however I am unsure how to turn it into a function or a for loop. I will therefore be going to office hours either Tuesday or Wednesday of next week to figure out how I can shorten this plotting code.

  • Example Feedback 2: It was recommended that I also look at the variability in the stock price of the top 50 companies as part of my analysis.

    • Example Response 2: While this could potentially make my results regarding the effects of volume trading on stock prices more interesting, the feedback provided here goes too far beyond the scope of my original research question. I appreciate the feedback, but I do not have the resources to start an entirely new research project.

Plan to Finish your Project#

Finally, with all of your feedback in mind–and the need to answer your research question and meet the criteria listed in the Rough Draft Guidelines–create a list of tasks to complete before you submit the final draft of your project.
